How it works

You will see differnt types of graphs containing two marked values.

Your task will be to estimate what percentage the smalled marked value is of the larger marked value.

The full experiment consists of 60 trials, with 20 trials per visualization type. It should take around 10-15 minutes, your answers will be recorded at the end!
